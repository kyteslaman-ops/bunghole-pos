# The Bunghole POS - Employee Guide

> Quick-start guide for bartenders and staff. Ask a manager if you get stuck.

---

## Getting In

1. Enter your **4-digit PIN** on the keypad
2. Tap **GO** (or just wait -- it auto-submits after 4 digits)
3. Your name shows in the top-right corner

To lock the screen when you walk away, tap the **lock icon** in the top-right.

---

## Taking an Order

### Find the item

- Tap a **category chip** at the top (Cocktails, Well, Premium, Beer, etc.) to filter
- Or type in the **search bar** to find anything fast
- Keyboard shortcut: press **/** to jump to search

### Add to cart

**Regular items** (beer, wine, snacks, merch): just tap the card. It goes straight to the cart.

**Bourbon pours** (Well, Call, Premium, Top Shelf, Rare): a modifier popup appears:

| Pour       | Size  | Price Impact |
|------------|-------|-------------|
| Shot       | 1.5oz | Base price  |
| Neat       | 2oz   | +33%        |
| Rocks      | 2oz   | +33%        |
| Tasting    | 1oz   | -33%        |
| Double     | 4oz   | +167%       |

Pick the pour style, then tap **Add to Order**.

**Cocktails**: a bourbon upgrade popup appears:

| Upgrade    | Extra | Examples                  |
|------------|-------|---------------------------|
| Well       | $0    | Standard                  |
| Call       | +$3   | Buffalo Trace, Makers     |
| Premium    | +$6   | Angels Envy, Woodford     |
| Top Shelf  | +$12  | Blantons, Weller          |
| Rare       | +$25  | Pappy, BTAC               |

### Special items (not on the menu)

Tap **"Special"** in the toolbar. Enter a name and price. It gets added to the cart like any other item.

---

## Working the Cart

The cart is on the right side of the screen.

- **Change quantity**: use the **-** and **+** buttons on each item
- **Add a note**: tap **+note** on any item (e.g., "no ice", "extra lime", "sub diet") -- the note prints on the receipt
- **Remove an item**: tap the **X** button
- **Clear everything**: tap **Cancel Order** at the bottom (this voids the order -- you'll need to give a reason)

---

## Checking Out

### Before you charge

If there's **alcohol in the order**, the yellow ID check bar appears:

1. Check the guest's ID
2. Tap **"Tap to Confirm ID Checked"** -- it turns green
3. You cannot proceed to payment until this is done

### Card or Cash

1. Tap **Card** or **Cash**
2. The tip screen appears -- 20% is pre-selected
3. Adjust the tip if the guest requests (None, 15%, 20%, 25%, or type a custom amount)
4. Tap **"Accept Charges"**
5. Done! A receipt preview pops up

### Receipt options

- **Email Receipt**: tap to enter guest's email
- **Done**: dismiss and move on

---

## Splitting a Check

When a party wants to split:

1. Tap the purple **Split** button (next to Card/Cash)
2. Choose a mode:
   - **Even Split**: divide the total equally (pick 2, 3, 4+ ways)
   - **By Item**: assign each item to a specific ticket (drag items to Ticket 1, Ticket 2, etc.)
3. Tap **"Start Paying Tickets"**
4. For each ticket: pick Card or Cash, add tip, tap Accept
5. Repeat for each ticket until all are paid

---

## Room Charges

If a guest wants to charge to their room:

1. Add items to the cart
2. Tap **"Room Charge"** at the bottom
3. **Step 1**: Pick the room from the grid (occupied rooms are highlighted)
4. Tap **"Next: Add Tip"**
5. **Step 2**: Select tip amount, have the guest **sign on screen**
6. Tap **"Accept Charges"**

---

## Running a Tab

### Start a tab

1. Add the first items to the cart
2. Tap **"Start Tab"** (left side of cart header)
3. Enter the guest's name or room
4. Tap **Start Tab** -- items are saved, cart clears

### Add to an existing tab

1. Tap **"Open Tabs"** to see all active tabs
2. Tap **"+ Add"** on the tab you want -- it loads back into the cart
3. Add new items
4. Tap **"Start Tab"** again to save, or close the tab out

### Close a tab

1. Tap **"Open Tabs"**
2. Tap **"Close"** on the tab -- items load into the cart
3. Check out normally (Card, Cash, Split, or Room Charge)

## Discounts

Buttons are above the cart items:

| Button   | What it does                          |
|----------|---------------------------------------|
| **10%**  | 10% off the whole order               |
| **20%**  | 20% off the whole order               |
| **%**    | Custom percentage (you pick the %)    |
| **Comp** | 100% off -- manager comp              |

The discount shows as a green line in the totals area.

---

## Oops! Undo a Sale

After completing a sale, a yellow **UNDO** banner appears at the bottom for **60 seconds**.

- Tap **UNDO** (or press **u** on a keyboard) to reverse the sale
- Stock is restored, the transaction is removed
- After 60 seconds the banner disappears and you can't undo anymore

---

## 86'ing an Item

When something runs out:

1. Tap **"86 Mode: OFF"** in the toolbar -- it turns ON (red)
2. Tap any product card to toggle it as **86'd** (crossed out, unavailable)
3. Tap **"86 Mode: ON"** again to turn it off and go back to normal selling

86'd items show a red "86" badge and can't be added to orders.

---

## Clocking In / Out

Tap the **clock icon** in the top-right header bar.

- If you're clocked out, it clocks you in and says "Clocked in"
- If you're clocked in, it clocks you out and shows your hours

## Keyboard Shortcuts

| Key            | Action                              |
|----------------|-------------------------------------|
| **/**          | Jump to search                      |
| **Cmd+K**      | Jump to search                      |
| **Enter**      | Open card payment (if items in cart) |
| **Esc**        | Close any popup                     |
| **u**          | Undo last sale (within 60 seconds)  |
